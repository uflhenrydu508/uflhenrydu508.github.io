01 / About

About me

I am a Ph.D. student in Industrial and Systems Engineering at the University of Florida. My research focuses on learning causal relationships across subpopulations and environments, with interests in graphical models, nonconvex optimization, and clinical trajectory prediction.

Alongside my research, I have worked on fraud detection, credit risk, customer growth, and energy analytics. These experiences shape my interest in statistical methods that support practical, data-informed decisions.

02 / Research

Learning structure.
Understanding heterogeneity.

My research spans causal discovery, latent subpopulations, and longitudinal health data.

01

Multi-environment causal learning

Identifying shared relationships and environment-specific variation using a knowledge-informed framework with shared structure, sparse deviations, and proximal optimization.

Causal structure · Multi-environment data
02

Structure-aware clustering

Jointly learning sample clusters and group-specific causal graphs using structural equation models, DAG constraints, and ADMM-based optimization.

Heterogeneous data · Nonconvex optimization
03

Patient trajectory prediction

Combining soft-DTW temporal clustering with dynamic structural equation models to represent contemporaneous and time-lagged dependencies in ICU patient trajectories.

Healthcare · Time series
